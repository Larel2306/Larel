Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LAREL (“LAREL”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you visit our website, create an account, contact us or place an order.

In short: We use information to operate the store, authenticate accounts, process orders, arrange delivery and respond to customers. We do not sell personal information as a business practice.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: name and email address associated with an account. If you choose Google sign-in, authentication information is provided through the sign-in service.
  • Order and delivery information: items purchased, order status, recipient name, phone number and shipping address needed to fulfil and deliver an order.
  • Customer communications: information you include when contacting us about an order or support request.
  • Technical and usage information: basic device/browser data and website interaction information that may be collected by security, hosting or analytics technologies used on the site.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, maintain carts and orders, take steps to fulfil purchases, coordinate shipping, provide support, prevent fraud or misuse, improve the website, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Service providers and sharing

We may share the minimum information reasonably needed with providers that help operate the store. These may include Supabase for authentication and database services, Google when you choose Google sign-in, Qikink and relevant shipping partners for printing, packing, fulfilment and delivery, and hosting or website analytics providers. These providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect legitimate rights and safety.

We do not ask you to send your password to us by email. Payment details, where payments are handled by a third-party payment provider, are subject to that provider’s processing and privacy practices.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The website or its service providers may use cookies, local storage, session tokens or analytics technologies to keep sign-in working, remember preferences, protect the site and understand site usage. You can manage cookies through your browser, but disabling essential storage may affect sign-in or checkout features.

5. Data retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described above, including account operation, order support, accounting, dispute resolution and legal compliance. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to identity verification, records we must retain and applicable law. You can stop using Google sign-in at any time. Deleting an account may not immediately remove order records that we are legally or operationally required to keep.

7. Children

This store is not intended for children to make purchases without appropriate adult involvement. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Third-party links

Our website may link to third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites we do not control; review their policies before using them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website or practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the LAREL Contact page. Please do not include passwords or payment-card details in your message.
