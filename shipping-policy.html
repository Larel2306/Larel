Shipping Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Estimated delivery

Our current estimated delivery timeline is 7–8 days. This is an estimate and can vary by delivery location, production/fulfilment time, courier capacity, weather, public holidays and other circumstances outside our reasonable control.

2. Order fulfilment

LAREL orders may be printed, packed and fulfilled through our fulfilment partner, Qikink, and delivered by the applicable shipping partner. Fulfilment and tracking updates depend on the systems and information provided by those partners.

3. Delivery details

Please check that your name, phone number and full address, including PIN code and any useful landmark, are correct before placing an order. Delays or failed delivery caused by inaccurate or incomplete details may affect delivery time.

4. Tracking

If tracking information is available for your order, we will provide it through the order flow or the communication channel available to us. Tracking updates may take time to appear after dispatch.

5. Delays and delivery problems

If your order has not arrived within the estimated period, contact us through the LAREL Contact page with your order details. We will make reasonable efforts to check the status with our fulfilment or shipping partner.

6. Incorrect, damaged or missing orders

If an order arrives damaged, is incorrect, or appears to be missing, contact us as soon as possible and include your order number and clear photos where relevant. Any resolution will be assessed under our Refund & Return Policy and applicable law.

7. Contact

For shipping questions, use the LAREL Contact page.
