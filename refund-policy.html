Refund, Return & Exchange Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Change-of-mind policy: LAREL generally does not accept returns or exchanges and does not provide refunds for change-of-mind requests, incorrect size selection or a preference change after ordering.

1. No routine returns or exchanges

Please review the product description, size chart, colour and delivery details carefully before ordering. We do not offer routine returns or exchanges for change of mind, size selection, or simply because a customer no longer wants the item.

2. Refunds

As a general policy, orders are non-refundable once placed and processed, except where a cancellation is accepted by LAREL, a payment is duplicated or collected in error, an order cannot be fulfilled after payment, or a refund or other remedy is required under applicable law. Where a refund is due, it will be processed through the applicable payment method or payment provider, subject to that provider’s processing timelines.

3. Damaged, defective, incorrect or undelivered orders

Our no-return/no-exchange policy does not remove rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded. If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect item, or your prepaid order is not delivered, contact us promptly so we can investigate and determine an appropriate remedy under applicable law.

4. How to report a problem

  • Contact us through the LAREL Contact page as soon as reasonably possible.
  • Include your order number, a description of the issue and clear photos of the item/package where relevant.
  • Keep the product and packaging until the issue has been reviewed. Do not send an item back unless we have provided instructions or applicable law requires it.

5. Order cancellations

Orders may enter production or fulfilment soon after being placed. Cancellation requests are not guaranteed and depend on the order status and whether fulfilment has started. Contact us as quickly as possible. This clause does not limit any cancellation or refund right provided by applicable law.

6. Contact

For a request relating to an order, use the LAREL Contact page and include your order number. Never send passwords or full payment-card details.
